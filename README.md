FJ2W2510210 USB 2.0 2MP Ultra Wide Angle Camera Module

FJ2W2510210 is a USB 2.0 2MP ultra wide angle camera module designed for embedded vision systems, industrial equipment, robotics and smart devices.

The module combines an OS02K10 CMOS sensor, 1920×1080 video output at 30fps, a 3.1mm fixed-focus lens and a 175° diagonal field of view.

Product Overview

The FJ2W2510210 is designed for applications that require a wide viewing angle, Full HD video and a simple USB connection.

With a 175° diagonal field of view, the camera can capture a broad area from a relatively short working distance. The fixed-focus optical design also provides a straightforward solution for embedded equipment.

175° Ultra Wide Angle

The 175° diagonal field of view is one of the main features of the FJ2W2510210.

A wide field of view can help capture a larger area when the camera is installed close to the target. This makes the module suitable for equipment where broad-area observation is required.

The specified object distance is 120mm, which can be considered during the mechanical design and camera mounting process.

Fixed-Focus Design

The FJ2W2510210 uses a fixed-focus optical configuration rather than an autofocus mechanism.

This design can be suitable for equipment with a defined working distance. It can also simplify integration where continuous autofocus is not required.

When designing the enclosure, the target distance and camera mounting position should be considered carefully.
